Easy Phones for Seniors: What to Look For

By Peter Xu · Updated November 2026

A phone is a lifeline: it connects older adults with family, doctors, and help in an emergency. But many modern phones are full of small icons and confusing menus. The right choice depends on what the person actually wants to do.

Decide on the main uses

  • Calls only: a simple flip phone or big-button phone is often best.
  • Calls and texts with family: a flip phone with a good keypad, or a simplified smartphone.
  • Video calls, photos, and apps: a regular smartphone set up with large text and a simple home screen, or a phone designed for seniors.

Features that matter

  • Loud, clear sound and a volume control that is easy to find. Check if it is compatible with hearing aids (look for an "M" and "T" rating).
  • Large buttons with clear numbers on keypad phones.
  • A bright, readable screen.
  • Speed-dial or photo-dial buttons for family members.
  • An emergency button on some models, which calls or texts chosen contacts.
  • Long battery life and an easy charging dock, so the phone is not left dead.

Making a regular smartphone simpler

You do not always need a special phone. Many smartphones have built-in accessibility settings:

  • Increase text and icon size.
  • Turn on a simplified or "easy" home screen mode if the phone has one.
  • Put only the needed apps on the first screen.
  • Add family contacts as favorites with photos.
  • Turn on emergency SOS and medical ID.

Check the plan

Some senior-focused phones come with a monthly service plan; others work with any carrier. Compare the total yearly cost, not just the phone price, and check coverage where the person lives.

Set it up together

Whatever you choose, set it up with the person, practice making and answering calls, and write simple instructions on a card. A phone that the person is comfortable with will be used.

Bottom line

Start from what the person needs to do. For calls only, keep it simple. For video and photos, a well-set-up smartphone with large text can work very well.
