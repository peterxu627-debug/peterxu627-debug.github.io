About

Easy Care Guide is a small independent site by Peter Xu. It offers plain-language guides to everyday products that help older adults live safely and comfortably at home, and help family caregivers make good choices.

The guides are based on research, manufacturer information and widely shared safety advice. They are not lab tests, and they are not medical advice. For health questions, please talk with a doctor, pharmacist or occupational therapist.

Peter also publishes large-print planners, log books and puzzle books, listed on the Books page.
